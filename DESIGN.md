## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-minimalist, high-end editorial architecture inspired by elite branding consultancies and graphic design ateliers in New York and London. It merges the rigorous structural clarity of Swiss Modernism (Vignelli, Rams) with the quiet restraint of contemporary European luxury publishing.

The UI communicates uncompromising authority, anti-marketing radical transparency, and architectural precision. It eschews generic SaaS tropes (heavy gradients, pillowy rounded corners, bright neon accents) in favor of expansive negative space, disciplined layout grids, hairline delineation, and high-contrast editorial typography.

A core brand signature is the deliberate all-lowercase convention across micro-labels, section signposts, and major headlines, countered by disciplined sentence-case treatment for literary blockquotes and structural legibility.

## Colors

The palette operates on a deep, matte velvet canvas (`#0A0A0C` and `#121216`), devoid of blueish digital backlights, mimicking premium uncoated archival paper in reverse.

Primary typographic presence is commanded by warm off-white (`#F5F2EB`) and aged parchment cream (`#D4CEC3`), avoiding cold, clinical digital whites. Architectural structural lines rely exclusively on translucent off-white hairlines (`rgba(245, 242, 235, 0.08)`).

Accents are deployed with strict discipline:
- `accent-steel` (`#8CA3C4`): Employed for technical indicators, active structural lines, diagrammatic elements, and metadata.
- `accent-amber` (`#E8A33D`): Reserved strictly for high-value focal highlights, critical interactive alerts, or selective price emphasis.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs high-contrast transitional serif display treatments (`Playfair Display`) with an uncompromisingly clean, geometric sans-serif (`Plus Jakarta Sans`) for technical data, metadata, and body copy.

Editorial rules:
- **Case conventions**: Display headers and section categories adhere strictly to intentional lowercase styling (`# tu negocio es mejor de lo que parece.`, `## sobre nosotros`).
- **Quotes**: Master-quote typography (`quote-editorial`) uses sentence casing with italicization permitted only for author attributions.
- **Index numbering**: Numerals in process sequences use tabular zero-padded structures (`01`, `02`, `03`) set via `label-mono-num`.
- **Text contrast**: Avoid dense walls of text. Preserve line lengths strictly between 45 and 68 characters for editorial readability.

## Layout & Spacing

The design system is structured on a strict 12-column architectural grid with generous exterior margins. White space operates as an active, structural luxury element rather than void space.

Responsive behaviors:
- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column grid, `4rem` outer margin, `2rem` gutter. Generous section separation using `space-xl` (`6rem`) or double step increments (`12rem`) for major topical transitions.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column grid, `2.5rem` outer margin, `1.5rem` gutter. Service comparisons reflow into structured 2-column modules.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column grid, `1.25rem` outer margin, `1rem` gutter. Sequences stack linearly; numerics (`01`, `02`) float directly above step titles rather than in adjacent lateral columns.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects blurry drop shadows and heavy multi-layered ambient elevations. Visual hierarchy is established via flat tonal stratification and crisp structural boundaries:

- **Level 0 (Canvas)**: `#0A0A0C` background, completely flat.
- **Level 1 (Panels & Surfaces)**: `#121216` background framed with a microscopic 1px hairline boundary (`rgba(245, 242, 235, 0.08)`).
- **Level 2 (Active/Hover Cards & Drawers)**: `#18181E` surface with lightened border tone (`rgba(245, 242, 235, 0.16)`).
- **Floating Modals & Drawers (Brandi Assistant)**: Grounded by a backdrop-filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px); background-color: rgba(10, 10, 12, 0.88);`) bounded by a subtle perimeter stroke. Depth is tactile, architectural, and glass-planed rather than illusionistic and pillowy.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly sharp (`roundedness: 0`), reflecting high-end architectural and industrial design rigor. 

Every visual perimeter—buttons, card containers, inputs, toggle panels, pricing tables, and drawer modals—features razor-sharp right angles (`border-radius: 0px`). Precision and geometry replace casual consumer-app softness. Visual delicacy is achieved through microscopic 1px hairlines and balanced inner padding rather than corner radiuses.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary Action**: Sharp rect, filled off-white background (`#F5F2EB`), deep matte text (`#0A0A0C`). Hover shifts to steel blue (`#8CA3C4`) text with off-white background or inverts to a crisp hairline perimeter.
- **Secondary Action**: Sharp rect, transparent background, 1px hairline border (`rgba(245, 242, 235, 0.12)`), text `#F5F2EB`. Hover shifts border to `#F5F2EB`.
- **Text / Inline Triggers**: Prefix or suffix anchors using structural punctuation: `[ + portfolio ]`, `+ ver condiciones`, or `↑ volver arriba`.

### Cards & Grid Containers
- Built on `#121216` with zero radius and 1px hairline stroke (`rgba(245, 242, 235, 0.08)`).
- Spacing inside cards is uniform and generous (`space-md` to `space-lg`).
- Hover state: Subtly brightens border to `rgba(245, 242, 235, 0.2)` without physical scale shifting or elevation lifting.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: Flat `#0A0A0C` or transparent background with a continuous bottom hairline or 4-sided sharp border. Placeholder text in `#D4CEC3` at 40% opacity. Focus state transitions the stroke cleanly to `#8CA3C4`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Square 16px geometric indicators with sharp edges. Check state marked with an internal sharp glyph or solid fill.

### List Items & Micro-Indicators
- Problem/Solution items pair binary typographic icons:
  - Negative: Red or faint cream `✕` with faded descriptor.
  - Positive: Clean `✓` with bold, direct atelier statement.
- Section step sequences feature prominent zero-padded numerics (`01`, `02`, `03`) set in `label-mono-num` stacked vertically or laterally aligned via hair-line separators.

### Pricing Matrices & Data Tables
- Strict architectural grid lines with zero visual fluff or bloated cards.
- Clear itemized deliverables with hairline dividers (`border-bottom: 1px solid rgba(245, 242, 235, 0.08)`).
- Direct, unadorned pricing displays without psychological gimmicks (`.99`).

### Drawers & Conversational Assistants
- Right-aligned or modal slide-out panel utilizing surface `#121216` over blurred backdrop.
- Micro-labels denoting status: `brandi · asistente creativo` paired with an amber status dot and security signoff (`protegido por cloudflare turnstile`).
